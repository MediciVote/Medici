New here? How Medici works in 3 steps
  1. Get MEDICI with ETH, right on this page or in any trading app (see Get MEDICI below).
  2. Propose or vote for the idea you want built. Each week, the idea with the most votes is built and launched by the IMD swarm as a new token. One MEDICI burned is one vote. Proposing and voting both burn MEDICI, permanently lowering its total supply, and burns aren't refunded if an idea loses.
  3. Share in the winner.
    • Proposed the winning idea? You're paid an ETH bounty from the treasury when it launches, and your proposal burn counts as a vote for the backer share below.
    • Voted for it? You and the other backers split 5% of the new token's supply, in proportion to what each burned. Claim it on this page within 90 days.
    • Holding MEDICI? The treasury owns the fee share of every token Medici launches, so it earns 1% of all their trading, forever, on top of 1.5% of every MEDICI trade. It spends that money on future launches, proposer bounties, and buying back and burning MEDICI.

Propose an idea

Writing a brief the swarm accepts
  • Say what it does in plain words, then the exact numbers: fees, timings, who gets what. The swarm builds exactly what you write.
  • Say what nobody can do: no owner, no minting, no pause. Briefs without admin powers pass review more easily.
  • Keep it to one clear mechanic. The best past launches (games, fee-sharing hooks, NFT rewards) did one thing well.
  • No website: the swarm builds the contracts and tests.
  • IMD's own guide lists every field the swarm understands: imd.fun/docs (see "Open a job"). Past winners below link to their IMD job, so you can see what got built.
Fixing a flagged proposal.

Want ongoing income from your idea? If it wins you get the bounty and a backer share, but the new token's standard trading fees go to the Medici treasury. Your brief can pay you through its own mechanics instead, e.g. "0.5% of every trade, taken in ETH, goes to 0x…your wallet". Keep it modest: voters read it before they burn.

    Fair launch

    No dev allocation. The team kept no MEDICI for itself:

    • 90% of the supply went into the trading pool, single-sided, with no team tokens held back.
    • 10% went to the IMD swarm: split between every seat online at launch and the workers who built it. The team's one IMD seat got the same share as every other seat, about 0.01% of supply.
    • The contracts have no owner, no minting, no pause and no upgrade path.

    How the team is paid: fees only, set in the contracts and visible on-chain.

    • 0.5% of every MEDICI trade, paid in ETH: part of Medici's 2% fee. The other 1.5% goes to the treasury. The treasury's own buybacks don't pay this fee.
    • IMD's standard launch fee share: 1% of every MEDICI trade, as on every IMD launch. The ETH half goes to the team; the MEDICI half is burned automatically by a fee router contract, so the team never collects MEDICI. The handover to that router is permanent.
    • No other income. An automated keeper runs the weekly chores (checking proposals, settling weeks, sending winners to IMD). It holds no IMD and can take at most 0.01 ETH a week from the treasury, for gas only.

    How it works

    • Propose: fill in the form above. The week's first idea burns MEDICI worth 0.002 ETH (about what launching on IMD yourself costs), and each idea after it costs 15% more, so nobody can cheaply fill all 20 slots. The price uses MEDICI's 24-hour average, and the burn counts as your first vote. Your brief is stored on-chain with the proposal, so it can't be changed later. Up to 20 ideas a week, during the week's first 7 days.
    • Rules for briefs:
      • Ethereum only, traded against ETH or IMD.
      • A kind with a trading pool: hook, project or token.
      • 85% of the new token's supply goes into its pool, and 5% to the Medici treasury for backers.
      • A project's owner role goes to the burn address.
    • Vote: burn any amount of MEDICI for an idea. One burned MEDICI is one vote. Votes can't be moved or refunded.
    • Last hour: a vote in the final hour pushes the end back to 10 minutes after it, by at most 24 hours in total.
    • The winner: when the week ends, the idea with the most burned wins and the runner-up is kept as a backup. To launch, the winner needs burns worth at least 0.01 ETH at the week's average MEDICI price (shown live above the proposals). Below that, nothing is built that week.
    • Launch: the treasury sends the winning brief to IMD exactly as voted and pays IMD's 0.5 IMD fee itself. The IMD swarm then builds, tests, audits and deploys it as a new token with its own trading pool, usually within a few hours. Every proposal gets IMD's free check first (shown on its card); if IMD still refuses the winner, the runner-up takes its place automatically.
    • Rewards for the winner's backers: 5% of the new token's supply, split in proportion to what each burned for it (the proposer's burn included). Claim it under Past weeks within 90 days; what nobody claims is burned.
    • Reward for the proposer: an ETH bounty, paid when the launch goes live. It's the smallest of 10% of the treasury's free ETH, 1 ETH, and half the value burned for the idea, so it grows as the treasury does. A brief can also pay its proposer through its own mechanics, e.g. a small fee on every trade; voters read it before they burn.
    • Where the treasury's money comes from: 1.5% of every MEDICI trade, plus the fee share of every token Medici launches. The treasury is each launch's fee owner on IMD, so it earns 1% of all that token's trading, forever.
    • Where it goes: IMD's fee for each launch, the proposer bounties, and buybacks: 40% of each week's income buys MEDICI over the following day and burns it.
    • If a winner doesn't launch within 14 days, or is skipped because IMD refuses it, the runner-up gets the slot, judged on its own burns.
